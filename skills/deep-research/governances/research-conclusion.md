# Research Conclusion

`conclusion.md` is the default file consuming agents should read.

Each `conclusion.md` must be a complete, condensed verdict of the research and should stand on its own for most readers.

Required sections, in this order:

1. last updated (month and year; updated whenever the verdict changes materially)
2. question
3. verdict
4. **reference material** — any tables, diagrams, quick references, or "when to use" guides that make the verdict immediately actionable. Include everything a reader needs to act on the verdict without reading other files. If none, omit.
5. confidence
6. strongest support
7. strongest counterevidence
8. not supported
9. thin evidence
10. recheck triggers

Reference material comes before confidence and evidence so a reader hits the actionable content first, not the research methodology. Confidence and evidence sections are for readers who want to validate or challenge the verdict — they are not the primary read path.

Do not write `conclusion.md` as a teaser that requires opening `topic.md` to understand the result.
